why: The most repeated objection in the thread, and it misses the claim. Fine-tuning
  is about the laws and constants allowing stars, chemistry and life anywhere, not
  about life being common. Critics mostly dispute what the sensitivity means, not
  that it exists. Martin Rees, who prefers a multiverse to God, documents it in Just
  Six Numbers (1999). (One commenter went on to steel-man the argument correctly.)
