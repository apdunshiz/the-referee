question: q04
text: Statements about minds cannot be true independently of minds, so morality cannot
  be mind-independent.
side: unstated
verdict: MOSTLY FALSE
strength: 3
why: This mixes up two meanings of “objective.” In ethics, objective means true regardless
  of anyone's opinion, not “about non-mental things.” “Steve is in pain” is about
  a mind and still objectively true. The rock thought experiment (intent changes moral
  weight) is good, but it doesn't prove morality is subjective.
