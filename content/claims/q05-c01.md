  on several occasions.

why: 'The texts exist (1 Samuel 15:3; Deuteronomy 20:16–17; Joshua 6:21). This is
  the strongest challenge to tying morality to the God of the Bible, though not to
  the abstract moral argument. Christian scholars answer three ways: (1) “utterly
  destroy” is stock ancient war hyperbole, since the same peoples are alive later
  (Judges 1; Copan & Flannagan, 2014); (2) the giver of life has rights over it that
  humans lack; (3) revelation was progressive, with Jesus as the clearest picture
  of God. Christians still debate how well these work.'
