question: q08
text: The claim that the creed dates to within a few years exists only in the imagination
  of apologists.
side: unstated
verdict: MOSTLY FALSE
strength: 4
why: They're technically right that “pre-Pauline” only means older than the letter
  (c. AD 53–55), and Paul says he passed the creed on earlier (c. AD 50). But early
  dating isn't an apologists' invention. Gerd Lüdemann, a German New Testament scholar
  who publicly left Christianity, dated the tradition to within three years of the
  crucifixion (30–33 CE). The usual reasoning is that Paul met Peter and James in
  Jerusalem about three years after his conversion (Galatians 1:18–19). That's an
  inference, not a certainty.
