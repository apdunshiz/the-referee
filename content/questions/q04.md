title: Is morality objective, and does it need God?
confidence: 80
short: Open. Most philosophers accept objective morality, but most of them don't think
  it needs God.
verdict: Open. “Some things are objectively wrong” has majority support among philosophers
  but can't be proven. “Therefore God” is the weak link, because most moral realists
  aren't theists.
reply: 'Ask first: “Do you think torturing a child for fun would be wrong even if
  everyone approved?” If they say no, switch arguments. If they say yes: “Then what
  makes that true in a universe made only of particles? I think a good God explains
  it better than calling it a brute fact.”'
