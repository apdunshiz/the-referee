Sean McDowell's The Fate of the Apostles (2015), the study two commenters cite, rates the chance each was martyred. Highest: Peter, Paul, James son of Zebedee, and James brother of Jesus. More probable than not: Thomas. More plausible than not: Andrew. As plausible as not: seven others. Improbable: John. He says the key fact is their willingness to suffer, and that there are no early accounts of any apostle recanting.
