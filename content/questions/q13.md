title: Even if he rose, does that make Jesus God?

short: Not by itself. You need the argument that it vindicates what Jesus claimed
  about himself.
verdict: Your post skipped a step. The Christian argument is that the resurrection
  vindicates what Jesus claimed about himself, such as forgiving sins (Mark 2:5–10)
  and his “Son of Man” answer at trial (Mark 14:61–62). God wouldn't raise a man executed
  for blasphemy if those claims were false. Also, Elijah-type miracles were revivals,
  and those people died again. The Christian claim is a transformed body that never
  dies.
reply: “Agreed, a resurrection by itself doesn't make someone God. My claim is that
  it vindicates what Jesus said about himself, the claims he was executed for.”
