## The 22 proposed changes, by worldview

**Christianity (25 → 25)** [christianity.md](christianity.md)
- history 6→7: early, stable text (1 Cor 15 creed, Paul's letters, manuscript record) is the same kind of fact that earned Islam and Hinduism +1.
- consistency 5→4: foreknowledge beside free choice is charged to Judaism and Islam, so it is charged here too (Alex's ruling, 2026-10-28). Islam keeps the kalam bonus (same ruling): the arguments originated in Islamic thought and borrowers do not get the originator's credit.
- testability stays 2: the proposed held-promise credit (the church's endurance) is the continuous community already credited under history, the same double count removed from Judaism below. The "return within a generation" deduction stays (Ehrman, Allison, Fredriksen; the preterist reading is a minority and does not cover Paul).

**Buddhism (29 → 27)** [buddhism.md](buddhism.md)
- history 5→6: the sangha is a continuous, documented community from Ashoka's edicts; the other traditions got this point.
- consistency 7→6: no-self must also ground compassion; its best defenders (Williams, Siderits) have not closed that gap; mirrors naturalism's obligation deduction.
- testability 4→3: the Lotus Sutra and Japanese worldly-benefit practice promise answered prayer, so the prayer-trial deduction applies as it did to Hinduism.
- moral 6→5: the eight garudhammas subordinate nuns to monks in every Vinaya, an exclusion written into the structure, the same kind that cost Islam.

**Hinduism (26 → 24)** [hinduism.md](hinduism.md)
- history 7→6: the epic chronology (Kali Yuga 3102 BCE) and the Indus-Vedic continuity claim conflict with archaeology, linguistics and ancient DNA (Narasimhan 2019); Judaism and Islam lost a point for the same kind of fact.
- moral 6→5: legal coercion of converts (Nepal's statute from 1963; anti-conversion laws in about ten Indian states; Peshwa caste penalties) is the class Islam was debited for.

**Islam (22 → 25)** [islam.md](islam.md)
- history 5→6: a continuous, documented community (Dome of the Rock 691) was credited to three other traditions and not to Islam.
- explanation 6→7: the kalam and contingency arguments (al-Ghazali, Ibn Sina) are the distinctive-resource bonus the other traditions received.
- testability 1→2: the "uncorrupted text" claim was exposed by the Sanaa palimpsest and held.

**Judaism (28 → 24)** [judaism.md](judaism.md)
- consistency 7→6: foreknowledge versus free choice (Avot 3:15, Maimonides) is the tension that cost Islam a point. Alternative: drop Islam's charge instead.
- explanation 5→6: Job is one voice; Saadia, the Talmud and Maimonides give the test and recompense answers Islam was credited for.
- testability 3→2: "survival of the people" is the continuous community already credited under history; the rule forbids scoring it twice.
- moral 7→5: persecution by states built on it (Hasmonean forced circumcision, Himyar, Israel's 1948–66 military government) and exclusion in its structure (Lev 25:44–46 slavery, the verses that cost Christianity).
- history 6→5: the restated arithmetic (+1 community, +1 early stable text, −1 Exodus and conquest unsupported, −1 late legendary founder biography) reaches 5; the report's "keep 6" miscounted.

**Naturalism (30 → 29)** [naturalism.md](naturalism.md)
- consistency 6→5: truth-tracking reason from fitness-tracking selection (Nagel, Plantinga) is a third doctrine needing careful definition.
- explanation 5→6: physicalism is an answer to the minds question of the same kind as "the soul".
- testability 7→6: "mind tracks brain" is the brain science already credited under history; no double count.

**Deism (22 → 29, floor 25)** [deism.md](deism.md)
- history 5→6: rests on the same scientific record that earns naturalism +1.
- explanation 3→5: every classic deist text states a moral law known by reason; "the creator does not suspend lawful nature" is the suffering answer naturalism got.
- testability 2→4: predicts no miracles and null prayer trials, the two held kinds naturalism gets; −1 for organism design failing to Darwin. (Contestable.)
- moral 5→7: "no communities and no record" is false; the Jacobin state decreed deism in 1794 and ran the Terror under it, so it is scored on its record like the others. (Contestable.)

## Rule problems every report flagged (not scored)
1. The moral rule punishes traditions that held state power longer (scale bias); stateless stances get a free pass or a flat 5.
2. "Inherited cosmology counts as an answer for everyone" gives the lawful-cosmos point to traditions that explicitly refuse the question (Buddhism's unanswered questions).
3. Testability rewards exposure and then takes it back for every failed kind, so the most checkable tradition can score lowest.
4. History credits and debits the same facts across files inconsistently; each report found the same kind of fact scored differently in another file.
